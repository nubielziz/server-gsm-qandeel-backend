# Phase 3 pre-build audit: console endpoints versus the backend plan

Six Opus auditors compared every pending console endpoint (frontend-endpoints.json) with the endpoint docs, the decisions and the real Drizzle schema (2026-11-01). Each finding is to be resolved in the docs, the schema or a brief before its feature is built.

## identity-platform (49 endpoints; high 1, medium 9, low 6)

I checked 49 console endpoints for identity and platform against frontend-endpoints.json, the endpoint docs, the schemas and the fixtures; the other 35 pending keys have no console function. Most shapes, enums and codes line up. The real gaps are around merchant creation: POST /admin/clients has no owner for the wallet and credit limit and no route that uses the invite token. Users rule 2 (credit limit) is also missing from its errors. Other gaps: ROLE_PROTECTED versus LAST_OWNER, idle sessions still counted as live, audit actorName for merchant and customer actors, and api_keys /32. On the dashboard, sums mix currencies, the health words don't map, and the pastSla rule disagrees with B92. The small ones are notification audience mapping, webhook responseCode null, the settings allow-list versus deny-list, staff password status, the audit entityType list, and the merchant-portal routes that only have a map line.

## catalog (37 endpoints; high 3, medium 10, low 4)

I checked the 37 pending console endpoints of the catalog module. Shapes, enums, list queries and success statuses mostly match the schema and catalog.md.
Main gaps: B117's effective-cost floor is not carried into catalog.md, RULES or ANSWERS, so the admin rows, belowFloor, resolve and the 422 floor checks are unclear. Three documents describe the resolver (rounding, rule scopes) differently. POST /admin/services cannot insert because price_mode and duplicate_rule are NOT NULL with no default.
Several deletes and edits would hit FK or unique errors (500) the doc does not cover: deleting a service, a client group (pricing_rules), or a category (dangling rules), editing a TAC, and duplicate options, group prices or category names. Smaller nullability gaps: releaseYear, basePrice, batchRef.
