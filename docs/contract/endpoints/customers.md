## POST /admin/customers/:id/block

- **Map**: Block a customer for fraud, or lift the block, with a reason; blocking adds the customer fingerprint to the blocklist (use suspend for a temporary stop). (حظر عميل لاحتيال أو فك الحظر مع السبب، ويضيف بصمته للقائمة السوداء (للإيقاف المؤقت استعمل suspend)) · staff-permission `customers:block` · stage 18 · tables customers, blocklist_entries, audit_logs
- **Frontend**: `blockCustomer` (src/features/customers/api/accounts.ts:76) · params `id: number`, `body: BlockCustomerBody` · returns `void` · success 204 · assumed contract
- **Request**: Body {blocked: true, reason} or {blocked: false}.
- reason is one of 'Chargeback or dispute', 'Card testing or fraud', 'Abuse of refunds', 'Other' (lib/status.ts:55-60).
- {blocked: false} carries no reason; accept an optional one.
Block: sets status blocked and risk_level high. Adds the customer's email (hash, masked display) and known card fingerprints and devices to blocklist_entries, with source manual and the reason. Revokes sessions.
Unblock: sets status active, moves risk_level high to medium, and lifts the entries this block added.
Writes audit_logs both ways.
- **Response**: 204.
- **Errors**: 404 NOT_FOUND (no customer with that id); 409 ALREADY_BLOCKED (block on a blocked customer); 409 NOT_BLOCKED (unblock on a customer who is not blocked); 422 VALIDATION_FAILED (reason missing when blocking, errors[{field: 'reason'}]); 409 ANONYMIZED (the customer is anonymized); 403 FORBIDDEN (caller lacks customers:block)
- **B48**: The map stores the customer 'fingerprint', while the demo copy says the email (M73). Store the email hash plus payment fingerprints. The map says lifting is 'with a reason', but the console sends none: make it optional. The demo and mock overwrite internal_notes with the block reason (RiskTab shows Notes); proposal: append instead. Revoking sessions on block needs the sessions table, which is not in the route's tables. → Proposal accepted.
- **Mock**: src/features/customers/mocks/actionHandlers.ts (Refusal codes per state, the export file with Content-Disposition, and the side effects of anonymize on orders and requests.)

## GET /admin/customers/:id/export

- **Map**: Export the customer data for a data-protection request. (تصدير بيانات العميل بطلب حماية البيانات) · staff-permission `customers:privacy` · stage 18 · tables customers, orders, consent_records, data_requests
- **Frontend**: `exportCustomerData` (src/features/customers/api/privacy.ts:11) · params `id: number` · returns `string` · success 200 · assumed contract
- **Request**: :id. Side effects: marks the customer's open or in_progress export request completed (completed_at, handled_by) and writes audit_logs. It may also store the file and set data_requests.file_id.
- **Response**: 200 with a JSON file and Content-Disposition: attachment; filename=customer-<id>.json. The console falls back to that name when the header is missing; expose the header through CORS. Content: profile, orders (id, service, IMEI, status, totals, dates), payments without card data, consents, checkout sessions, support tickets with their public messages, and data requests. Works for every customer, anonymized ones included.
- **Errors**: 404 NOT_FOUND (no customer with that id); 403 FORBIDDEN (caller lacks customers:privacy)
- **B49**: The route's tables lack audit_logs (the demo logs 'exported data of') and the payment, checkout and ticket tables a complete export reads; add them. This GET has side effects: see crossCutting (CSRF). → Proposal accepted.
- **Mock**: src/features/customers/mocks/actionHandlers.ts (Refusal codes per state, the export file with Content-Disposition, and the side effects of anonymize on orders and requests.)

## POST /admin/customers/:id/anonymize

- **Map**: Anonymize the name, email and IMEI while keeping payment records for accounting. (إخفاء الاسم والإيميل والـ IMEI مع إبقاء سجلات الدفع للمحاسبة) · staff-permission `customers:privacy` · stage 18 · tables customers, data_requests, audit_logs
- **Frontend**: `anonymizeCustomer` (src/features/customers/api/privacy.ts:21) · params `id: number` · returns `void` · success 204 · assumed contract
- **Request**: No body. Irreversible.
- customers: status anonymized, anonymized_at now, name 'Deleted customer', email deleted-<id>@redacted.invalid, phone '', internal_notes '', marketing_opt_in false, has_account false.
- Deletes credentials and revokes sessions and tokens.
- Cuts stored IMEIs to the TAC plus a mask on the customer's orders, checkout sessions and IMEI checks.
- Keeps payments, ledger entries and amounts.
- Closes an open or in_progress erasure request (completed).
- Writes audit_logs.
- **Response**: 204. The console then refetches the customer, the data requests and the orders (hooks/usePrivacyMutations.ts:31-46).
- **Errors**: 404 NOT_FOUND (no customer with that id); 409 ANONYMIZED (already anonymized); 403 FORBIDDEN (caller lacks customers:privacy)
- **B50**: The route's tables are customers, data_requests and audit_logs. Removing the IMEI (map description) and what the console expects (orders refresh with a masked IMEI and buyer) also need writes to orders, checkout_sessions, imei_checks, sessions, customer_credentials and auth_tokens. Proposal: add them. → Proposal accepted.
- **Mock**: src/features/customers/mocks/actionHandlers.ts (Refusal codes per state, the export file with Content-Disposition, and the side effects of anonymize on orders and requests.)

## GET /admin/checkouts/abandoned

- **Map**: Abandoned carts, including who has marketing consent. (السلات المتروكة ومن عنده موافقة تسويق) · staff-permission `growth:read` · stage 19 · tables checkout_sessions, consent_records
- **Frontend**: `listAbandonedCheckouts` (src/features/checkouts/api/checkouts.ts:14) · params `query?: ListQuery` · returns `ListResult<AbandonedCheckout>` · success 200 · assumed contract
- **Query**: _start/_end, 12 per page. _sort createdAt|grossAmount|recoveryEmailedAt, default createdAt desc (lib/filters.ts:54-57, 105-115). 'Needs a reminder' sends canRecover=true; 'All' sends no filter. q: case-insensitive contains over the FULL email, since rows carry it masked (mocks/handlers.ts:63-69).
- **Response**: JSON array + x-total-count. Row:
- id.
- email: masked as 'ab•••@domain'; the console masks again, which leaves this format unchanged.
- grossAmount: USD, 4-dp, incl. VAT.
- fxRate: decimal string, currency units per USD; the console shows grossAmount x fxRate.
- currencyCode (currencies.code) and serviceName (services.name).
- status (checkout_sessions.status).
- utm: the stored jsonb; the console reads utm.source.
- createdAt.
- recoveryEmailedAt: null until a recovery email is sent, automatic or manual.
- orderId: set when the session was paid.
- marketingConsent: the latest marketing consent record for the session or customer is a grant.
- canRecover: the server's verdict.
- **Errors**: 403 FORBIDDEN (caller lacks growth:read)
- **B60**: Rows need services and currencies joins and the abandonedMinutes setting (for canRecover); the route's tables are checkout_sessions and consent_records. → Proposal accepted.
- **Mock**: src/features/checkouts/mocks/handlers.ts (Rows come from mocks/store.ts (demo S.checkouts and S.services; consent made up per id; the recovery word becomes recoveryEmailedAt, orderId and canRecover). See also test/fixtures.ts and api/checkouts.test.ts.)

## POST /admin/checkouts/recover

- **Map**: Send a recovery email once, only to customers with marketing consent. (إرسال إيميل استرجاع مرة واحدة لمن وافق على التسويق) · staff-permission `growth:send` · stage 19 · tables checkout_sessions, notifications
- **Frontend**: `recoverCheckouts` (src/features/checkouts/api/checkouts.ts:28) · params `body: RecoverCheckoutsBody` · returns `RecoverCheckoutsResult` · success 200 · assumed contract
- **Request**: Body {ids: number[]}: the ticked rows with canRecover, at most a page. The server checks each id again: exists, not already emailed, not paid, marketing consent granted, and due. Each eligible checkout gets one recovery email (a notification with a one-click link back to the saved basket) and recovery_emailed_at is set. Duplicate ids are ignored.
- **Response**: 200 with {sent, skipped: [{id, reason}]}. Reasons: no_marketing_consent|already_emailed|already_paid|not_found; any new code is shown with underscores as spaces. Check in the mock's order: not_found, already_emailed, already_paid, no_marketing_consent (mocks/handlers.ts:14-20). A send that reached someone toasts; any skip opens the summary dialog (M133).
- **Errors**: 422 VALIDATION_FAILED (empty or invalid ids, errors[{field: 'ids'}] (mocks/handlers.ts:39-40)); 403 FORBIDDEN (caller lacks growth:send)
- **B61**: audit_logs is missing from the route's tables (the demo logs 'sent recovery emails'). Re-checking consent at send time also reads consent_records. → Proposal accepted.
- **Mock**: src/features/checkouts/mocks/handlers.ts (Rows come from mocks/store.ts (demo S.checkouts and S.services; consent made up per id; the recovery word becomes recoveryEmailedAt, orderId and canRecover). See also test/fixtures.ts and api/checkouts.test.ts.)
